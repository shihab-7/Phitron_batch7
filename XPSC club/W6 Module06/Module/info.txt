The Goldbach conjecture is a famous unsolved 
problem in number theory that states every even 
integer greater than 2 is the sum of two prime numbers. 
For example, \(4=2+2\), \(6=3+3\), \(8=3+5\), and \(10=3+7\). 
It was first proposed by Christian Goldbach in 1742 and has been 
verified for very large numbers, 
but a formal mathematical proof is still missing.
